WHO WE ARE

Walking Together Towards a Better Society

Pather Sathi Amra Foundation is a community-driven initiative committed to social welfare, education and positive social change.

We believe that meaningful change begins when individuals come together with a shared purpose of helping others.

Our Vision

To contribute towards a more aware, supportive and compassionate society.

Our Mission

To encourage community participation and meaningful social initiatives.

Our Activities
