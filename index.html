WHO WE ARE

Walking Together Towards a Better Society

Pather Sathi Amra Foundation is a community-driven initiative dedicated to building an educated, conscious, compassionate and violence-free society. We believe that meaningful social change begins when people come together with a shared vision of humanity, equality and collective responsibility.

Through educational initiatives, social welfare, environmental activities and community support, we strive to create a positive impact and encourage every individual to contribute towards a better tomorrow.

Our Mission

To foster an educated, conscious, humane and violence-free society by promoting education, human values, social awareness and community welfare. We aspire to help every child grow through education, compassion, responsibility and dignity.

Our Vision

To build a peaceful, inclusive and enlightened society where every child has the opportunity to grow, every individual is guided by human values, and equality, kindness and mutual respect shape our collective future.

Our Objectives

  • Promoting education and the holistic development of children.
  • Encouraging humanity, kindness, equality and moral values.
  • Building a peaceful, conscious and violence-free society.
  • Raising awareness about social and community responsibilities.
  • Protecting the environment through collective action.
  • Supporting underprivileged and needy people.
